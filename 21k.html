91% singletrack. All singletrack except a mile of forest road (half dirt and half paved) and the final ¼‑mile paved sprint.

A figure-eight double loop from White Pines Park on the same rugged terrain as the long courses, through the Cowell and San Antonio drainages: technical singletrack, big trees and ridge views in a self-contained adventure. The natural step from road half marathons to trail.

Expect a wet crossing of San Antonio Creek.
