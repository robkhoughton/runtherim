Your sponsorship goes to the trail

Runner entry fees are set to cover the cost of putting on the race, so sponsorship goes to the Arnold Rim Trail: building and maintaining it for hikers, runners, mountain bikers and equestrians. The Arnold Rim Trail Association is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.
