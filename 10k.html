80% singletrack. All singletrack except a mile of forest road (half dirt and half paved) and the final ¼‑mile paved sprint.

A fast, friendly loop from White Pines Park through a big-tree conifer forest, across the San Antonio drainage and out onto the Arnold Rim Trail itself. The step up from the 5K: twice the distance and real climbing, still well within reach in your first trail season.

Expect a wet crossing of San Antonio Creek.
