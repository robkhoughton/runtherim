Getting there

  • 50K and 35K start: Fullen Road, Arnold Rim Trail Post 100. A shuttle takes runners from White Pines Park to the start.
  • 21K, 10K and 5K start: White Pines Park.
  • Every distance finishes at White Pines Park, 1965 Blagen Rd, Arnold, CA (at Blagen Rd and Dunlap Rd).
  • Directions: From Highway 4, turn left on Blagen Road, then left on Dunlap Road.
  • Parking: Hazel Fisher Elementary School lot, Dunlap Rd, Arnold.

Time limit

All runners must be off the course by 5:00 PM. Aid-station cutoffs are listed in the Runner Guide.

Timing

Timing by OnYourMark Events.

After the race

Post-race food, with plenty of carbs, is served at the finish from 11:00 AM, with vegetarian options. Send dietary requests to info@arnoldrimtrail.org. There are also sweet and salty snacks and fresh fruit.

Dogs

Dogs are welcome in White Pines Park and on the Arnold Rim Trail. They must be leashed at all times in the park. If you run with your dog, keep it on leash, start at the back of the pack, and let every other runner go first.
