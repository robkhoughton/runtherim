The race

Run the Rim is presented by the Arnold Rim Trail Association in partnership with and under permit from the Calaveras Ranger District, Stanislaus National Forest. It runs on the Arnold Rim Trail in the Sierra Nevada foothills of Calaveras County. Most of the route is single track through pine, fir and cedar forest, with long views off the volcanic rim.

Choose from five distances:

  • 50K — the longest course: point to point from Fullen Road (Post 100) to White Pines Park, with 9 more miles than the 35K.
  • 35K (22 miles) — the classic end-to-end Arnold Rim Trail, Post 100 to Post 1.
  • 21K (half marathon) — a figure-eight double loop on the same rugged terrain as the long courses.
  • 10K and 5K — fast, friendly loops around White Pines Lake on smooth single track. The 5K is the perfect first trail race.

All distances finish at White Pines Park, and the lake is right there for a post-race swim.

Every entry builds the trail

Run the Rim is a fundraiser for the Arnold Rim Trail Association, the volunteer nonprofit that builds and maintains the Arnold Rim Trail for hikers, runners, mountain bikers and equestrians. Your registration goes straight back into the trail.
