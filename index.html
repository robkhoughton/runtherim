The race

Not every trail race crosses an ancient lava cap. Run the Rim follows the Arnold Rim Trail through the Sierra Nevada foothills of Calaveras County, right next door to Calaveras Big Trees State Park: singletrack through big trees of ponderosa pine, sugar pine and incense cedar, up to the volcanic rim at 4,500 feet, with views west to Mount Diablo and east to the Sierra crest.

Run the Rim is presented by the Arnold Rim Trail Association in partnership with the Calaveras Ranger District, Stanislaus National Forest.

Five distances, one finish line. Start where you are and step up: each distance takes you farther onto the trail, from your first trail race around White Pines Lake to a full day on the rim.

  1. 5KGatewayThe perfect first trail race: a loop around White Pines Lake under big trees. All the beauty, none of the vert.
  2. 10KQuarter HorseYour first miles on the Arnold Rim Trail itself, with a wet crossing of San Antonio Creek.
  3. 21KHalf MarathonA figure-eight on the same rugged terrain as the long courses. The natural step from road half marathons to trail.
  4. 35KART ClassicThe Arnold Rim Trail end to end, Post 100 to Post 1, up onto the rim. Your bridge to the ultra.
  5. 50KUltraThe ART Classic plus 9 more miles of our finest singletrack: a real introduction to mountain ultras.

Every distance finishes at White Pines Park, on the shore of White Pines Lake. Jump in.

Every entry builds the trail

Run the Rim is a fundraiser for the Arnold Rim Trail Association, the volunteer nonprofit that builds and maintains the Arnold Rim Trail for hikers, runners, mountain bikers and equestrians. Event proceeds go straight back into the trail system.
