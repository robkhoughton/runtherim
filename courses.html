Courses

Five distances, one finish line. Quality singletrack nearly all the way, and every distance ends the same way: ¾ mile of paved singletrack, then a ¼‑mile paved sprint to the finish at White Pines Lake.

Aid-station cutoffs will be published in the 2027 Runner Guide.
