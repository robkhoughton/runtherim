Courses

Five distances on the Arnold Rim Trail. Every course finishes at White Pines Park.

50K35K21K10K5K

Aid station types

  • Full: everything at Limited, plus soda, carbs and calories
  • Limited: water, HEED, electrolytes and gels
  • Water: water only

Aid-station cutoffs are listed in the Runner Guide.

  • Start: Fullen Road, Post 100 (shuttle)

21K

8:30 AM start
  • 13.5 miles
  • 2,000 ft elevation gain
  • Start: White Pines Park

A figure-eight double loop from White Pines Park on the same rugged terrain as the long courses.

The course crosses a creek, so expect wet feet.
