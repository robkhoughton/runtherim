50K Ultra

7:00 AM start
  • 31.4 miles
  • 5,900 ft elevation gain
  • Check in: White Pines Park (mandatory shuttle)

The longest course: point to point along the Arnold Rim Trail to White Pines Park, with 9 more miles than the 35K. Expect a full day: sustained climbs out of three Sierra drainages, the open volcanic rim at 4,500 feet, then long, soft stretches of pine-needle single track through ponderosa pine, sugar pine and incense cedar.

35K ART Classic

8:00 AM start
  • 22.2 miles
  • 4,900 ft elevation gain
  • Check in: White Pines Park (mandatory shuttle)

The classic: the Arnold Rim Trail end to end, Post 100 to Post 1. Punishing rocky descents, then the payoff: long, flowing pine-needle single track so fast and soft you forget you've been climbing. From the rim, the views run west to Mount Diablo and east to the Sierra crest.

21K Half Marathon

8:30 AM start
  • 13.5 miles
  • 2,000 ft elevation gain
  • Start: White Pines Park

A figure-eight double loop from White Pines Park on the same rugged terrain as the long courses: technical single track and ridge views in a self-contained adventure. The natural step from road half marathons to trail.

The course crosses a creek, so expect wet feet.

10K Step Up

8:45 AM start
  • 6.2 miles
  • 700 ft elevation gain
  • Start: White Pines Park

A fast, friendly loop from White Pines Park through mixed conifer forest and out onto the Arnold Rim Trail itself. The step up from the 5K: twice the distance and real climbing, still well within reach in your first trail season.

The course crosses a creek, so expect wet feet.
