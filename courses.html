50K Ultra

7:00 AM start
  • 31.4 miles
  • 5,900 ft elevation gain
  • Check in: White Pines Park (mandatory shuttle)

91% singletrack. Everything but 2.5 miles of groomed forest road and the final ¼‑mile paved sprint.

Ultramarathon: point to point along the Arnold Rim Trail to White Pines Park, with 9 more miles of our finest singletrack than the 35K. Expect a full day: sustained climbs out of three Sierra drainages (San Domingo, Cowell and San Antonio), the open volcanic rim at 4,500 feet, then long, soft stretches of pine-needle singletrack through big trees of ponderosa pine, sugar pine and incense cedar.

35K ART Classic

8:00 AM start
  • 22.2 miles
  • 4,900 ft elevation gain
  • Check in: White Pines Park (mandatory shuttle)

99% singletrack. All singletrack except the final ¼‑mile paved sprint.

The classic: the Arnold Rim Trail end to end, Post 100 to Post 1, across three drainages: San Domingo, Cowell and San Antonio. Punishing rocky descents, then the payoff: long, flowing pine-needle singletrack under big trees, so fast and soft you forget you've been climbing. From the rim, the views run west to Mount Diablo and east to the Sierra crest.

21K Half Marathon

8:30 AM start
  • 13.5 miles
  • 2,000 ft elevation gain
  • Start: White Pines Park

91% singletrack. All singletrack except a mile of forest road (half dirt and half paved) and the final ¼‑mile paved sprint.

A figure-eight double loop from White Pines Park on the same rugged terrain as the long courses, through the Cowell and San Antonio drainages: technical singletrack, big trees and ridge views in a self-contained adventure. The natural step from road half marathons to trail.

Expect a wet crossing of San Antonio Creek.

10K Quarter Horse

8:45 AM start
  • 6.2 miles
  • 700 ft elevation gain
  • Start: White Pines Park

80% singletrack. All singletrack except a mile of forest road (half dirt and half paved) and the final ¼‑mile paved sprint.

A fast, friendly loop from White Pines Park through a big-tree conifer forest, across the San Antonio drainage and out onto the Arnold Rim Trail itself. The step up from the 5K: twice the distance and real climbing, still well within reach in your first trail season.

Expect a wet crossing of San Antonio Creek.

9:00 AM start
  • 3.1 miles

92% singletrack. Everything but the final ¼‑mile paved sprint.

A loop around White Pines Lake, and the perfect first trail race: smooth, fast singletrack under big trees. All the beauty, none of the vert.
